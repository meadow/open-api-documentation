---
title: Checkout Handoff
description: Send customers to a Meadow checkout page to complete their order
---

Checkout Handoff lets you build your own shopping experience and hand the customer off to Meadow to check out. Meadow's checkout page handles pricing, discounts, loyalty, payment and creating the order, so you do not need to integrate payments or loyalty yourself.

Checkout Handoff requires the **Create Orders** [permission](/permissions). Orders placed through Checkout Handoff are attributed to your integration, the same as orders created with [POST /api/v1/orders](/endpoints/orders/create-order).

## Step 1 - Create a cart

When the customer is ready to check out, create a cart with [POST /api/v1/carts](/endpoints/carts/create-cart). Use product option IDs from the [menu](/endpoints/menu/list-menu).

```javascript
{
  "type": "pickup",
  "userId": 4821,
  "returnUrl": "https://example.com/checkout/complete",
  "lineItems": [
    {
      "productOptionId": 1899,
      "quantity": 3
    }
  ]
}
```

If the customer changes their cart before being redirected, you can replace its line items with [PUT /api/v1/carts/\{cartId\}](/endpoints/carts/update-cart).

## Step 2 - Redirect to the checkout page

Redirect the customer's browser to the `checkoutUrl` from the response, from a page on the dispensary's authorized domain. It will look like:

```
https://embed.getmeadow.com/organizations/{organizationId}/h/checkout/{cartId}
```

The customer reviews their order, pays and places it on the Meadow page. A cart can only be checked out once.

## Step 3 - Return to your site

If you set a `returnUrl`, the customer is sent back to it after checkout. By default Meadow shows its order confirmation page with a link back to your site. If the dispensary turns on automatic redirects, the customer is sent straight back instead. Without a `returnUrl`, Meadow shows its order confirmation page.

## Step 4 - Look up the order

Retrieve the cart with [GET /api/v1/carts/\{cartId\}](/endpoints/carts/retrieve-cart). Once the customer has checked out, `orderId` will be set. Use it with [GET /api/v1/orders/\{orderId\}](/endpoints/orders/retrieve-status) to follow the order's status.

# Dispensary settings

Dispensaries configure checkout handoff in Meadow under **Settings > API Integrations > Checkout Handoff**:

- **Authorized domain** (required) - checkout handoff is turned off until the dispensary sets this to your domain. Until then, creating a cart returns `CHECKOUT_HANDOFF_NOT_CONFIGURED`. Once set, `returnUrl` must be on this domain or one of its subdomains (otherwise creating the cart returns `INVALID_PARAMS`), and customers must be sent to the checkout page from a page on this domain (see [Security](#security)).
- **Automatically redirect to the return URL** - off by default, and only available once an authorized domain is set. When on, customers are sent straight back to `returnUrl` after placing their order.
- **Appearance** - the colors, corner radius and button style of the checkout page.

# Customer vs. guest carts

### Customer carts

Pass `userId` with the ID of a customer in the organization (for example, from [GET /api/v1/customers/\{identifier\}](/endpoints/customers/retrieve-customer)). The order will be placed for that customer, and pricing will include their discounts and loyalty eligibility.

### Guest carts

If `userId` is omitted, the order is placed as a guest order using `customerName`. Guest carts must have a `type` of `pickup` (creating a guest `delivery` cart returns `INVALID_PARAMS`), and the organization must have anonymous pickup orders enabled for the cart to be checked out.

# Security

<Warning>
  Treat `checkoutUrl` as a secret bearer link. Anyone with it can check out the cart as that customer.

  - Do not log it or share it anywhere other than the customer's browser
  - Create the cart right before redirecting the customer, not ahead of time
</Warning>

The checkout page only opens when the browser's referrer is on the authorized domain. Browsers send it by default, so do not open `checkoutUrl` with `rel="noreferrer"` or from a page with `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, otherwise the customer sees "Checkout unavailable".

This check only stops other sites from sending customers to checkout pages. It does not protect a leaked `checkoutUrl`.

Carts stop working if the dispensary deletes your integration or removes its **Create Orders** permission: the checkout page shows "Checkout not found" and the cart can no longer be priced or checked out. Create a new cart once access is restored.
