# Checking out

When the customer checks out, redirect their browser to the cart's `checkoutUrl`. It opens the checkout of the dispensary's menu with the cart, where the customer signs in or checks out as a guest and places the order. See the [Checkout Handoff guide](/guides/checkout-handoff) for the full flow.
